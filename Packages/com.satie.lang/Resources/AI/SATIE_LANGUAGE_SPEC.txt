# SATIE LANGUAGE SPECIFICATION

## SYNTAX RULES

### Commands (NO colons, NO quotes)
loop audio/file
oneshot audio/file every 2to5
5 * oneshot audio/file every 1to3

### Properties (space-separated, NO equals unless legacy)
volume 0.5
volume 0.1to0.8
pitch 1
pitch 0.5to1.5
fade_in 2
fade_out 1to3

### Numbers and Ranges
- Single value: 0.5 or .5 (both work)
- Range: 0.1to0.8 or .1to.8 (NO SPACES around "to")
- ALWAYS use "to" for ranges (NOT "-" or "..")

### Movement
move walk
move fly
move pos 5 0 10

### Visuals
visual trail
visual sphere
visual cube
visual trail and sphere

### DSP Effects
filter mode lowpass cutoff 2000
filter mode highpass cutoff 5000
reverb wet 0.5 size 0.8
delay wet 0.6 time 0.4 feedback 0.3

### Interpolation
goto(AandB in seconds)
gobetween(AandB in seconds)
gobetween(AtoC andDtoE in seconds)

Examples:
volume goto(0and0.2 in 0.5)
pitch gobetween(0.1to0.5and1.5 in 2to4)
filter mode highpass cutoff gobetween(5000and15000 in 5)

### Groups
group name
volume 0.5
    loop audio/file
        volume 0.3
    oneshot audio/file every 2
        pitch 0.8to1.2

### Flags (standalone, no value)
randomstart
overlap
persistent
mute
solo

## COMPREHENSIVE WORKING EXAMPLE

comment
This is a complete, tested example demonstrating all major Satie features.
Study this carefully - this is the correct, current syntax.
endcomment

group birds
    volume 0.2
    3 * oneshot animals/birds every 2to5
        volume 0.1to0.3
        pitch 0.8to1.2
        move fly
        visual trail
endgroup

group ambient
    volume 0.15

    loop ambience/forest
        volume 0.1
        filter mode lowpass cutoff 2000

    loop ambience/rain
        volume 0.05
        fade_in 3
endgroup

group bicycles
    5 * oneshot bicycle/10 every 1to1.5
        volume goto(0and0.2 in 0.5)
        pitch gobetween(0.1to0.5and1.5 in 2to4)
        randomstart
        delay wet 0.9 time 0.5to0.9 feedback 0.2to0.5
        filter mode highpass cutoff gobetween(5000and15000 in 5)
        reverb wet gobetween(0.1and1 in 10) size 0.8
        move fly
        visual trail and sphere
        color gobetween(255and0 as inquad in 2)
endgroup

group movement_examples
    oneshot animals/cow2 every 3to5
        move walk
        visual cube

    oneshot animals/birds every 2to3
        move fly
        visual trail

    oneshot prepared-piano/1 every 1to2
        move y -10to10 speed 1
        visual = cube
endgroup

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ loop "ambience/forest"  → ✅ loop ambience/forest
❌ volume = 0.5            → ✅ volume 0.5
❌ every 2-5               → ✅ every 2to5
❌ move = fly              → ✅ move fly
❌ 0.5 to 1.0              → ✅ 0.5to1.0 (NO SPACES)
❌ pitch 0,5               → ✅ pitch 0.5 (use DOT not comma)
